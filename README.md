# Qatar Cars Data Visualization

A data visualization project exploring car prices in Qatar using the **Qatar Cars** dataset from TidyTuesday.

## Goals

* Explore how a car's country of origin influences its price.
* Compare electric, hybrid, and petrol vehicles based on price and horsepower.
* Determine whether vehicle size and trunk volume are strong predictors of price compared with origin and engine type.

## Dataset

**Qatar Cars** — TidyTuesday (December 9, 2025)

The dataset contains information about international car brands sold in Qatar, including price, country of origin, engine type, horsepower, size, mass, and trunk volume. Prices are measured in Qatari Riyal (QAR).

Source: [TidyTuesday – Cars in Qatar](https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday/tree/main/data/2025/2025-12-09)

## Analysis

The project uses:

* **R**
* **tidyverse** for data manipulation and visualization
* **ggplot2** for charts
* **PCA (Principal Component Analysis)** to explore relationships between price and vehicle characteristics

### Key Findings

* Country of origin is strongly associated with car price in the Qatari market.
* EVs are not more expensive than petrol for equivalent horsepower.
* Larger vehicle size does not necessarily mean a higher price. Brand origin appears to have a stronger relationship with price than physical size alone.
